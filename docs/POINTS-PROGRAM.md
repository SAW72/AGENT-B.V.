# Agent-BV (Agent Bot Verifier) Points
Superseded. The points rules are in [AGENT-BV-POINTS-SPEC.md](AGENT-BV-POINTS-SPEC.md). Points are an off-chain record of activity only. They are not a token, have no cash value, can't be sold, transferred or redeemed, may be adjusted or cancelled at any time, and do not convert to anything. No token, airdrop, conversion, snapshot or claim is planned.
